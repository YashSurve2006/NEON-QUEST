How to play

🎯 Goal

Choose ▶ Play for an endless run, or pick a campaign level in Level select. The campaign has 3 levels, ending with the Stone Golem boss.

You start with 4 lives. Lose them all and the run is over. Campaign levels can be retried; an endless run starts again from 0.

∞ Endless runs

  • Each stage is a new level with a flag at the end. Clear it to move on to the next, forever.
  • Stages come from six worlds (Sunny Meadows, Frozen Caverns, Volcano Keep, Dune Sands, Neon Skyline, Cloud Isles) and eight obstacle mixes. The sign at the start of a stage tells you its mix.
  • Every run shuffles worlds and mixes, so no two runs look the same. Stages get harder as you go.
  • Run over (no lives left): your score goes to the high scores and a new run starts at 0.

⚔️ Moves

  • Stomp: land on an enemy's head to squash it. Stomp again before touching the ground for a combo (x2 up to x5).
  • Dash: a quick, invincible burst. It smashes walkers, bats and turrets, and damages the Golem. It has a short cooldown.
  • Coyote time & jump buffer: you can jump a few frames after leaving a ledge or press jump slightly early.

🧭 The world

  • Slime: walks in a patrol. Stomp or dash it.
  • Bat: swoops in waves. Stomp or dash it.
  • Turret: fires at you when near. Dash into it to smash it.
  • Golem rocks and fireballs: dodge or dash through them.
  • Spikes: hurt you. Jump over them.
  • Water and lava pits: falling in costs a life and sends you to your checkpoint.
  • Coins: +10 points. Every 30 coins gives a bonus life.
  • Hearts: +1 life.
  • Spring pads: launch you high. Stand on them.
  • Checkpoints: save your spot after you pass them.
  • Moving and crumbling platforms: crumbling ones fall after a moment, so keep moving.
